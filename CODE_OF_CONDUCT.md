# Code of conduct

GameForge is a small hobby project. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in this repository, is welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, also when reporting bugs or reviewing code.
- Assume good intent, and criticize code and ideas, never people.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the project, and explain your view when you disagree.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, and offensive remarks about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Requests for games, ROMs or keys, or links to them. GameForge is for backups of games you own.
- Spam and repeated off-topic posts.

## Reporting

Use **Report content** in the menu (···) of the issue, pull request or comment and choose to report it to the repository maintainers. Reports are read only by the maintainer and handled privately.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people from the repository who do not follow this code, depending on how serious and how repeated the behavior is.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
